The point is not to settle everything at once. The point is to make a next step visible.

What the scholarship says

Claim
The poster’s meaning shifts when it is placed in historical and cultural context.
The image is familiar, but familiarity is not the same as understanding.
Method
Research asks us to move from assumption to evidence.
That movement is what distinguishes inquiry from mere observation.
Source
Kimble and Olson (2006) provide the historical framing for the poster’s meaning.
The point is not only what the image says, but how we come to know it.

Kimble & Olson, 2006

Assumptions and biases

Research is also about noticing what we bring to the page.

  • What do I assume?
  • What am I taking for granted?
  • What would change if I asked another question?
