Roll Call

Attendance

As we call roll, answer one quick question:

  • Are you more of a morning person or a night owl when it comes to writing?
  • What's the last thing you read that wasn't for school?
  • What's a word or phrase your family uses that no one else would understand?
  • What's the last thing you wrote that had nothing to do with school?
  • What class are you most excited or nervous about this semester?

The Fine Print

Policy Highlights

Attendance: four absences allowed before the grade drops.
Grading: State Writing Requirement course — C- or higher required to pass.
Late Work: 48-hr notice grants an extension; otherwise a 5%/day penalty, then a zero after 10 days.
AI Use: permitted only when explicitly assigned; disclosure required.
Disability Access: contact Student Accessibility Services (SAS) to arrange accommodations.

Communication is key!!!

Next Class

Readings

  • Nathalie Singh-Corcoran, "Composition as a Write of Passage" (Writing Spaces)
  • Kara Taczak, "The Importance of Transfer in Your First Year Writing Course" (Writing Spaces)

Due

  • Reading Reflection #1 (1–1.5 pages) — Choose one misconception about writing Taczak names, trace where that belief came from, explain how Taczak's revised truth challenges it, and name one habit or skill that will transfer forward into this class.
  • ePortfolio Work Session (5 pts, in class) — Choose your ePortfolio platform and create your site. By the end of class you should have a live URL and a working home page — credit is for progress, not polish.
